Methodological Innovation — EVT Cap (69.26%) Capping Layer
01. Theoretical Foundation (GPD / POT)
Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) Peak-Over-Threshold (POT) modeling (McNeil & Frey 2000).
02. Mathematical Formula & Cap Definition
σ̂t+1 = min(LightGBM(Xt-1), EVT_Cap)
EVT_Cap: Capping value set at 69.2614% (Annualized) to mathematically limit extreme outlier forecasts.
03. Why Capping Matters & Model Collapse Prevention
Prevents LightGBM from extrapolating unobserved extreme volatility spikes during black swan crashes (e.g., March 2020 Liquidity Crunch / November 2022 Crypto Contagion), preventing model collapse.

Risk Engine — Filtered Historical Simulation (FHS)
Volatility-Adjusted Bootstrapping & Portfolio VaR/ES Estimation
01. Standardized Residuals
e_t = (r_t - μ_t) / σ̂_t
Isolates independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) empirical residuals by dividing daily returns by conditional volatility.
02. 1-Day 95% VaR Formulation
VaR_0.95 = -Quantile_0.05({σ̂_t+1 · e_τ}) × Portfolio Value
Projects tomorrow's maximum potential loss at a 95% confidence level by scaling contemporary volatility forecasts with historical residuals.
03. Expected Shortfall (CVaR 95%) & FHS Advantage
CVaR_0.95 = E[L | L ≥ VaR_0.95] Expected Shortfall (CVaR) measures the average loss beyond the 95% threshold.
FHS Advantage: Captures empirical asymmetry, skewness, and fat tails directly from historical residual distributions.
